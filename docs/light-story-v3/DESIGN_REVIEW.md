# Critical review, 23 November 2026

Verdict: not ready for publication. The latest local rewrite is not deployed to Sites or the primary domain. The existing hosted review URL still contains v2.

The owner explicitly requested a critical judgment during implementation. Desktop and 390px mobile were inspected in the exact static export. Build, TypeScript and lint passed; those checks do not establish design quality.

## Keep

- Inline alternating compositions: first text left/prism right, second reversed, continuing through six chapters.
- Personal narrative drafted by the explicitly requested copywriting agent, in JOURNEY_STORY, with distinct archival/research/speaking/writing/telescope photographs.
- Removal of the pinned player-like visual band, floating numbered controls and repeated generic biography block.

## Blocking visual findings

1. The restored original prism shader is hazy. Its procedural glass is a visible improvement over the wireframe but still does not match the crisp photographic reference. Do not call this photorealistic or approved.
2. Optical imagery is still separate scene instances in each section. Alternating their position does not provide the requested continuous passage of the same light across compositions. That transition architecture needs to be resolved, not concealed by more headings.
3. Large vertical gaps break the narrative, including the mobile transition into the early-exhibition chapter. Keep purposeful breathing room while reducing empty section boundaries.
4. Desktop detector framing clips the screen in the narrower inline canvas. Camera distance was tuned for the former wide viewport and needs to respect the inline aspect ratio.
5. Several photos need source-specific framing. The final telescope photograph currently prioritizes sky/trees and hides the person in the visible crop. Preserve the person and instrument; do not apply one generic crop to every image.
6. Copy is more personal, but the origin paragraph begins by describing the photograph. Rewrite it around a supported moment or motivation, without inventing specifics from AI-drafted About copy. Headline and paragraph length still need editing for scanability.
7. The ending should resolve the visual journey and invite contact. It currently returns to repeated selected-work/writing/marketing blocks, weakening the ending.

Mobile document width did not overflow at the tested 390px viewport. No claim of physical iPhone Safari testing is made. Evidence includes qa/journey-v3/02-reversed.jpg and mobile-judgment.jpg.

Next implementation should address scene continuity, glass material, aspect-aware framing and photo crops before another review deployment. Main astrokshitij.com remains untouched.
